## Asset acceptance rules

A new asset may be mapped to production only after:
1. Product identity is unambiguous.
2. Asset is specific to that product/variant.
3. Image/video is not reused for an unrelated product.
4. File path/URL is verified.
5. At least 2 distinct images and 1 video are available for the product before marking it media-complete.
6. Existing verified assets are preserved.
7. Production audit confirms product-to-media mapping after mutation.
